FIREBASE COLLECTIONS
=====================
1. donors/
   - Stores donor profiles
   - Fields: phoneNumber, fullName, bloodGroup, age, location, 
            lastDonationDate, totalDonations, fcmToken, isAvailable

2. emergency_alerts/
   - Stores emergency blood requests
   - Fields: hospitalName, requiredBloodGroup, contactNumber, 
            location, postedBy, createdAt, isActive, respondingDonors
